The Power of Unicode and Empty Text

Computers and modern applications understand text through standardized encoding systems known as Unicode. While a regular space is treated just as a separating keystroke, Unicode contains specific whitespace characters—such as the Em Space or En Space—that have physical code points recognized by systems as actual characters, even though they render entirely blank visually.
